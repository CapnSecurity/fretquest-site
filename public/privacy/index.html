FretQuest Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

FretQuest ("the app") is a guitar theory training game. This policy explains what information the app uses and how.

Information we collect

We do not run any servers for this app, we do not have user accounts, and we do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal information ourselves.

The app saves the following on your device only: your score and streak, your practice settings (such as note spelling and focus notes), and whether you have purchased the ad-free upgrade. This data stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

The free version of FretQuest shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob and Google may collect and use certain information to show and measure ads, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, and how you interact with ads. This is controlled by Google, not by us. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required by law, such as in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your Android device's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

In-app purchases

You can remove ads with a one-time purchase. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether you own the purchase.

Children

FretQuest is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Uninstall the app to delete everything it stores on your device.
  • Purchase the ad-free upgrade to stop all ads.
  • Use your device's ad settings to limit ad personalization.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
